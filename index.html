Four reasons. Four letters. One person I chose to write all of this for.

Dear Katy,

I don't think I say this enough at all, but thank you for all the times that you helped me with school. There are days when everything feels overwhelming, confusing, or just exhausting, but alot of times somehow you make it feel a little less heavy.

Whether you help me understand something, listen when I'm stressed, give me advice, or simply stay there when I need someone, you make a big difference in my life jud huhuhu. You remind me that I don't have to figure everything out alone.

Because of you, school doesn't always feel like something I have to face by myself, pero tungod sad nimo im facing these challenges right now hshshs, but i know its worth it rjud. Dear My one and only baby,

Sometimes my mind gets way too loud. I overthink, I worry, and I can let little things become much bigger in my head. You have a way of helping me slow down and come back to myself.

You remind me to breathe, think clearly, and not let one bad moment control everything. You don't have to solve every problem for me. Sometimes, just having you there is enough to help me feel steady again.

Thank you for being someone who helps me keep my balance and my composure, especially when I feel like I have neither. With you, I can be a little calmer, a little more patient, and a lot more at peace.

Thank you for being my calm in the chaos. ♡

Dear Cutie,

You motivate me in ways you probably don't even realize. Sometimes it's through your words, sometimes through the way you believe in me, and sometimes simply because I know there's someone cheering for me.

When I doubt myself, you remind me that I can still try. When I'm tired, you give me a reason to keep going. You make me want to improve, not because I have to be perfect, but because I know I'm capable of becoming better.

Thank you for reminding me of what I'm capable of. I hope I can give you even a little of the encouragement you've given me.

Thank you for believing in me. ♡

Dear you, my partner until the future,

And finally, the question: why did I choose you?

Honestly, it isn't because you're perfect. You're not, maskin ako huhuhu but fr. 😭 You hurt me, you tease me, and sometimes you really do keep kasaba-ing me. HAHAHA.
